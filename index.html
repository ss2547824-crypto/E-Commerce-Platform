The House

Meet the Owner

AR

Arjun Raghavan

Founder & Executive Chef

Arjun trained in Lyon and Mumbai before returning to Chennai to open Maison Saffron in 1998. His cooking begins with his grandmother's recipe notebook — slow masalas, stone-ground spices and produce sourced from farms within 200 kilometres.

Over 26 years the kitchen has served more than a million guests and holds a place on India's Top 50 Restaurants list. Arjun still tastes every batch of the house dal before service.

“Luxury is not decoration. It is patience — in the stock, in the spice, in the service.”
